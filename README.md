SOLMAR BAY is an open-world crime game in the browser: a sun-soaked coastal city to roam on foot or behind the wheel, where you can steal cars, weave through traffic and pedestrians, and outrun the police as your wanted level climbs. Five story missions, on-foot and drive-by combat, and a full HUD with minimap round out the life of crime. Built with three.js 0.187.0, no build step, no network at play time.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: WASD to move and drive, mouse to look, click to shoot, E to enter, exit or steal a car, F to punch, R to reload, Space to jump or handbrake, Shift to sprint, H to honk, Esc to pause. Walk into a green marker to start a mission.
